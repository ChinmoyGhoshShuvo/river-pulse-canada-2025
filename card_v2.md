**Title:** Canada's River Pulse 2025

**Summary:** I animated every mapped river in Canada for every day of 2025 as 3D walls whose height is daily discharge and whose colour is estimated water temperature. The animation combines HydroRIVERS, GloFAS and NASA POWER data, rendered with PyQGIS. Total flow to the sea swings 5.7-fold, from about 39,100 m³/s in February to about 221,100 m³/s at the end of June.
